Where do you start?

Lesson 0 · Monday 09:30 · Where you are, and a setup that works

≈ 45 minutesPrerequisite: noneReference: glossary
Why this lesson By Friday you'll have built a medallion pipeline on GCP, mostly alone: nobody's in the room Tuesday to Thursday. So you need:
  • a clear idea of where you start, so the hints and the stretch tiers fit your level
  • a working environment by 10:15, so the week goes into the pipeline, not Docker

1. Seven criteria, three levels core

Rate yourself. No grades, the answers just shape the week.

  • None: the hints in every exercise are written for you.
  • Some: you've used it but couldn't teach it.
  • Solid: you could explain it to your neighbour, so the stretch exercises are yours.

Paste the summary line in the Teams channel. The instructor uses the ten lines to pair you up, and on Friday you'll compare.

Done when your summary line is in the Teams channel.
